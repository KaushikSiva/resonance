How it works

Sees

The scene

Captures the screen several times a second. Reds, oranges and yellows count as warm; blues, cyans and icy whites count as cold. A hard cut fires a vibration click.

Senses · Infineon

Your breathing

The 60 GHz radar measures your chest moving from about half a metre away, with nothing to wear. It filters out the desk and walls, combines 3 antennas and tracks your breaths per minute.

Feels · DataFeel

Heat & cold on skin

The scene sets a target skin temperature from 28 to 38 °C, and each breath out nudges it warmer. The LED glows orange or blue and pulses with your breath.

Run the demo

  1. Strap the DataFeel dot on and sit about 50 cm in front of the radar. Start ThermoScene and drag a box around the video player above, then press Enter. It remembers the box next time. 
    cd ~/thermoscene && .venv/bin/python thermoscene.py --pick
  2. Give the radar about 30 s to show RADAR live, then press play.
  3. Feel the lava, feel the ice, then breathe slowly and feel the dot follow.
